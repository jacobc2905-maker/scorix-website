Scorix Verso

Water-powered desert guardian of Aquara DUNE

Scorix Verso is a water-powered desert hero from Aquara DUNE, a wasteland city once sustained by underground water. As the land became dry and dangerous, Scorix became a guardian powered by hidden water energy, survival, and transformation.

His world is filled with scorching heat, abandoned structures, shifting sand, and fading memories of survival. Through hidden strength, Scorix represents adaptation, resilience, and power hidden beneath destruction.

Scorix Verso Animation Project

This animation was created in Adobe After Effects and focuses on bringing Scorix Verso to life through movement, glowing water energy, and animated character details.
